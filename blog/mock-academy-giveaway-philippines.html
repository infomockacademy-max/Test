The Mock Academy Giveaway Philippines gives visitors an opportunity to try a simple spin-and-win experience. Instead of completing a long registration process before playing, the giveaway page lets visitors spin the prize wheel directly.

The page is designed around a quick three-step process: spin the wheel, reveal the result and complete the claim process.

How Does the Mock Academy Giveaway Work?

The giveaway uses a digital prize wheel. When you press the SPIN button, the wheel rotates and displays the result through the giveaway interface.

The current wheel contains eight visual sections, including cash prize sections and gift-image sections. The page's configuration identifies the cash prize as ₱500.

How to Join the Giveaway

1. Open the Giveaway Page

Start by opening the Mock Academy Giveaway page. The main page displays the giveaway wheel together with the spin button and information about the promotion.

2. Spin the Wheel

Press the SPIN button in the centre of the wheel. The wheel animation runs before displaying the giveaway result.

According to the current page setup, visitors can only spin once. The site also stores the completed spin status in the browser so that the wheel becomes unavailable after the attempt.

3. Check Your Result

Once the wheel stops, a result window appears. The current setup displays a congratulations message and identifies the prize as ₱500.

4. Complete the Claim Form

If you receive a claimable prize, the page asks for basic details through the claim form. The current form requests your full name and mobile number.

How Long Does the Spin Take?

The wheel animation is designed to last several seconds before the result window appears. This creates the visual experience of a physical prize wheel while keeping the process simple on both desktop and mobile devices.

Can You Spin Again?

The current website configuration is designed for one spin per browser session. After a completed spin, the page records the completed state and disables the spin button.

This means you should check your result carefully and follow the claim instructions shown on the page rather than expecting to receive another spin immediately.
